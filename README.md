## Why VerseUp

Most people who own a Bible want to read it more than they do. The text can feel long and unfamiliar, it is hard
to know where to start, and many Bible apps are built around accounts, adverts, subscriptions or a constant
internet connection.

VerseUp was built to remove those obstacles, so that anyone, anywhere, can open the Bible and keep reading:

- **Easy to start.** Scripture is shown a few verses at a time on cards you flick through, so a chapter never
  feels like a wall of text. When you want more, switch to a continuous page.
- **Easy to understand.** The Bible's great stories are gathered into illustrated, introduced stories you can
  read in order, and any word can be traced back to the Hebrew or Greek behind it.
- **Easy to keep going.** Reading plans, a Daily Verse, reminders and saved progress help reading become a habit.
- **Easy to hear.** A natural-sounding narrator reads Scripture aloud, right on your phone, so you can listen on a
  walk, in the car or with the screen off.
- **For everyone.** No account, no adverts, no subscription and no internet needed for reading. VerseUp is free,
  and it stays free.

## What VerseUp promises

- **Free, always.** No adverts, no subscriptions, no paid tiers.
- **Offline first.** The whole Bible is on your phone. Reading, stories, plans, notes and search all work with no
  connection. Narration voices and the study library are a one-time download, and then work offline too.
- **No account needed.** Open the app and read.
- **Scripture is never altered.** VerseUp uses the King James Version (1769 standard text). Paragraph breaks are
  added to make it easier to read; the words are unchanged. Stories, art, plans and study notes all point to the
  verses themselves, never to a rewritten version of them.
- **Your data stays yours.** Highlights, notes, bookmarks and progress are kept on your phone, and you can back
  them up to a file you control.

## What's inside

### Read

- **The whole King James Bible, offline.** All 66 books.
- **Cards or Continuous.** Read verse cards you flick through (a quick flick turns the card), or switch to
  **Continuous** to read a whole chapter as one page. VerseUp remembers where you were in either view.
- **Verses per card.** Choose how many verses each card holds, or let **Auto** fit them to your text size.
  Cards break at natural sections, so a thought is never cut in half.
- **Section headings** in every book, to help you see where you are.
- **The words of Jesus in red.**
- **Hold a verse** to highlight it, bookmark it, add a note, copy it or study its words.
- **Immersive view** hides the status bar for distraction-free reading.

### Stories

- **593 Bible stories**, from Creation to Revelation, each with a short introduction that sets the scene.
- **Illustrated.** 189 stories have their own artwork, and more are added with each release. In VerseUp's art,
  God and Jesus are shown as light rather than with a detailed face.
- **Parallel accounts.** Where the Gospels tell the same event, you can read each account side by side.
- **Journeys and collections.** Read the stories in Bible order, in chronological order, by era of history,
  through the life of Jesus, by theme (including the miracles and the parables), or start with a beginner's
  selection.
- **Progress for every story.** VerseUp remembers where you stopped in each story and which ones you have
  finished, and offers to start a finished story again.
- **Swipe sideways** to go to the next or previous story in the journey you are following.

### Listen

- **The VerseUp narrator** reads Scripture aloud in a natural voice, generated right on your phone, not streamed.
  Choose from four narrators: **Clara, Ellis, Rowan and Ivy**.
- **Offline.** The voice is a one-time download (a Standard pack, or a larger High quality pack). After that it
  works with no internet.
- **Keep listening anywhere.** Reading continues with the screen off or in another app. Control it from the lock
  screen, your headphones or the notification, with the story's artwork shown as the cover.
- **Android Auto.** Browse and play stories and Bible chapters from your car's screen, including by voice.
- **Read-along player.** A small player follows you around the app and opens into a full player, tinted by the
  art of the story you are hearing. When a story is heard to its last word, VerseUp marks it finished.
- **Phone voice option.** Prefer your phone's built-in voice? Switch to it in Settings.

### Reading plans

- **40 reading plans**, from a few days to a full year. Some examples:
  - Start Reading the Bible, Who Is Jesus?, The Life of Jesus, The Four Gospels
  - The Bible in 90 Days, 180 Days or One Year, Old and New Testament Together, The Chronological Bible
  - Psalms in 30 Days, Proverbs in 31 Days, The Parables of Jesus, The Miracles of Jesus
  - Faith Over Fear, Finding Peace in Difficult Times, Forgiveness and Grace, Learning to Pray, Heroes of Faith,
    Women of the Bible
- **Progress and catch-up.** See how far you have come, and catch up gently if you miss a day.
- **Reminders** at the time you choose, with quiet hours.
- **Read in the normal reader.** Each day's reading opens right in the Bible, with a bar that shows your plan.

### The Daily Verse

- **A verse for each day**, chosen from a collection of 240 verses across eight themes, each with a short
  reflection.
- **Home screen widget** so the day's verse is there whenever you unlock your phone.
- **Optional daily reminder** at a time you pick.
- **Backgrounds**: a sunrise valley, parchment, the art of a story, or a shuffle of them.

### Word study

- **Tap a word** to see the Hebrew or Greek word behind it, what it means, how it is used, and how to say it.
- **Interlinear view** of the original Hebrew and Greek with the King James text.
- **Grammar** for each original word, explained in plain language.
- **People and places** of the Bible, linked to the stories they appear in.
- **Cross-references** to related verses.
- The study library is a one-time download, then works offline.

### Saved: highlights, bookmarks and notes

Everything you highlight, bookmark or write a note on is gathered in **Saved**, so you can come back to it.

### Search

Search the whole Bible by word or phrase, or jump straight to a reference such as `John 3:16`. Search sits in a
round button at the bottom right of the screen, always within reach.

### Make it yours

- **Colour themes**, including a true-black theme for OLED screens, and light, dark or system mode.
- **Reading backgrounds**: a soft gradient or plain paper.
- **Fonts**: Literata or Roboto, with your choice of text size and line spacing.
- **App icon**: red or white.
- **Reduced motion and battery saving**: animations are shortened or switched off when you ask, or when your
  phone is saving power.

### Backup and restore

- **Back up to a file** (`.verseup`) that holds your highlights, notes, bookmarks, story and plan progress, and
  settings.
- **Protect it with a password** if you want; the file is then encrypted.
- **Restore on any phone**, either merged with what is already there or replacing it.
- **Automatic backups** on a schedule you choose.
- No account and no cloud are involved: the file is yours to keep wherever you like.

### Your first time in VerseUp

- **Welcome to VerseUp** asks what brings you to the app and a few reading preferences, then sets things up for you.
- **A short guided tour** on the real screens shows you how to flick cards, open stories, listen and save. Anything
  you try during the tour can be kept or undone at the end.
- **Tips** appear the first time you open a feature, and never for something you already use.
- **Discover VerseUp** in Settings lets you take the tour again at any time, and new features are announced when
  you update.

### Updates

VerseUp tells you when a new version is ready (see [Updating](#updating)). Every update is optional.

## Install on Android

VerseUp needs **Android 8.0 or newer**.

1. On your phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/henryneo/VerseUp-Releases/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, tap `VerseUp-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk` to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**
   (Android asks this once for apps that don't come from Google Play).
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

The other files in a release are for VerseUp itself and for checking the download: `update.json` tells the app
about the new version, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

> **Already using a test build?** Test builds and releases are signed with different keys, so Android won't
> install one over the other. Make a backup first (**Settings > Backup & restore**), uninstall the test build,
> install the release, then restore your backup.

## Updating

You only install VerseUp by hand once. After that:

- VerseUp checks this page for a new version in the background (about twice a day at most), and shows a short
  message when one is ready, with what's new.
- Tap **Update** and VerseUp downloads the new version, checks it, and hands it to Android, which asks you to
  confirm. Your highlights, notes, bookmarks and progress are kept.
- Tap **Maybe later** to be reminded in a few days. You can also check at any time in **Settings > App updates**.

Before installing, VerseUp verifies that the download is complete, that its checksum matches the release, that
it is a newer version, and that it is signed with the same key as the copy already on your phone. If any check
fails, nothing is installed.

## Checking a download

If you want to check an APK yourself, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one in the release's
`SHA256SUMS.txt`.

- **Windows (PowerShell):** `Get-FileHash .\VerseUp-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk -Algorithm SHA256`
- **macOS:** `shasum -a 256 VerseUp-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk`
- **Linux:** `sha256sum VerseUp-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk`

Only download VerseUp from this repository's [Releases](https://github.com/henryneo/VerseUp-Releases/releases)
page.

## Privacy

- **No account, no tracking, no adverts, no analytics.** VerseUp collects no personal data.
- Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, progress and settings are stored only on your phone, and in backup files you
  choose to make.
- VerseUp uses the internet only to download the narration voices and the study library when you ask for them,
  to load story artwork, and to check this repository for updates.
- Permissions on Android: **notifications** (only for reminders you turn on, and the playback controls),
  **playing audio in the background** (for narration), **running reminders after a restart**, **internet** (for
  the downloads above) and **installing updates** (Android always asks you to confirm).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is VerseUp really free?**
Yes. No adverts, no subscriptions and no paid features, now or later.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
Not to read. The Bible, stories, plans, notes and search all work offline. You need a connection once to download
the narration voice or the study library, to load story artwork the first time, and to check for updates.

**Which translation does VerseUp use?**
The King James Version (1769 standard text). The app is built so that more translations can be added later.

**Why isn't VerseUp on Google Play?**
For now, Android releases are published here. VerseUp updates itself from this page, so you only install it by
hand once.

**Will I lose my notes when I update?**
No. Updates install over the existing app and keep everything. Making a backup now and then is still a good idea.

**Android says the app can't be installed.**
This usually means a test build or an older copy signed with a different key is already installed. Back up,
uninstall it, then install the release and restore your backup.

**How do I report a problem or suggest something?**
Open an [issue](https://github.com/henryneo/VerseUp-Releases/issues) in this repository and include your VerseUp
version (shown in **Settings > App updates**) and your phone model.
